Companion For Mistria is an unofficial fan-made reference app for the game Fields of Mistria, published by Pickled Games (Michael Isasi). This SERVICE is free on iPhone and is intended for use as is.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies if anyone decided to use our Service.

Information Collection and Use

The app does not collect names, email, contacts, or location, and it does not collect an advertising identifier (IDFA). It does not track you across apps or websites, does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt, and does not include ads SDKs. There are no accounts, advertising, or crash reporting.

Anonymous usage analytics can be sent to [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com). This includes which screens and features are opened (for example gift lookups, checklist items marked, festivals opened, and calendar season changes), app sessions, and search text typed in the app (lowercased, at most 50 characters) with result counts. TelemetryDeck receives a hashed, anonymized install identifier, so the data is not linked to your identity. It is used only to understand how the app is used and to improve it.

You can turn this off at any time in the app: More tab > "Share anonymous usage data". When it is off, nothing is sent.

Data is processed by TelemetryDeck under its [privacy policy](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/). TelemetryDeck GmbH is based in Augsburg, Germany.

The App Store review request uses Apple's built-in system prompt. No data is collected by it.

Data on Your Device

Checklists (donated, caught, have) and settings such as the in-game date are stored only on your device (UserDefaults). They are never sent to the developer. Deleting the app removes them.

Images and Links

Item and character images load over HTTPS from the Fields of Mistria Wiki (fieldsofmistria.wiki.gg). Like any web request, that server may see the device's IP address under the wiki's own privacy policy. The app sends no identifiers. Links open in Safari.

Children’s Privacy

No children’s data is knowingly collected.
