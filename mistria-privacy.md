Companion: Fields of Mistria is an unofficial fan-made reference app for the game Fields of Mistria, published by Pickled Games (Michael Isasi). This SERVICE is free on iPhone and is intended for use as is.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies if anyone decided to use our Service.

Information Collection and Use

The app collects no personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

Data on Your Device

Checklists (donated, caught, have) and settings such as the in-game date are stored only on your device (UserDefaults). They are never sent to the developer. Deleting the app removes them.

Images and Links

Item and character images load over HTTPS from the Fields of Mistria Wiki (fieldsofmistria.wiki.gg). Like any web request, that server may see the device's IP address under the wiki's own privacy policy. The app sends no identifiers. Links open in Safari.

Children’s Privacy

No children’s data is knowingly collected. There is none to collect.
